# Aether — Survive in a Realm of Floating Islands

Aether 9minecraft Mod adds a massive new paradise dimension made of floating islands. Instead of traveling down to the fiery Nether, you travel up to a bright world filled with unique blocks, creatures, and giant dungeons. The mod gives you a completely fresh survival adventure with its own tools, armor, and bosses to defeat.

The Heavenly World: Builds a whole new dimension out of floating islands set under a bright blue sky.

## What this build is

An unofficial rebuild of **Blodhgarm**, **alphamode**, **baguchi**'s original work, kept running on current Minecraft releases by 9Minecraft Studios. Not affiliated with the original author and not endorsed by them.

Most of the porting work sits in the mixin layer. Each Minecraft release moves or renames the methods this mod hooks into, so the hooks are re-pointed and re-tested against that version rather than rewritten. The behaviour underneath is upstream's and is deliberately left alone — that is the part that makes the mod feel right, and it is not ours to redesign.
